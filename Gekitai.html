📜 Gekitai Rules

Classic Mode:

Gekitai is a 2-player strategy game played on a 6×6 board.

Each player has 8 pieces.

Phase 1: Players place pieces until all pieces are placed.

Phase 2: Players move their pieces to empty squares.

Pushing Rule: After placing/moving, adjacent pieces (up/down/left/right) are pushed one square away. Chain pushing is allowed.

If a piece is pushed out of the board, it is removed.

Win Conditions:

✅ Make 4 pieces in a straight line

✅ OR push all opponent pieces out

Challenge Mode (Extra Rules):

⛰ Blocked Cells: 4 random mountains. Cannot place/move there.

⚡ Trap Cells: 2 trap cells freeze the piece for 1 turn.

🧊 Frozen Pieces: Cannot be moved for 1 turn (but can still be pushed).

AI Mode:

🤖 You can play against computer. AI uses scoring-based decision making.
